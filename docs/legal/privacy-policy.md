# Privacy Policy for Bity

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**
**Effective date: 23 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what Bity ("Bity", "the app", "we", "us") does with your information. Bity is a calorie and nutrition diary for iPhone and iPad.

If you have any question about this policy, write to **bity.calorie.tracker@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. The short version

- **Bity has no accounts.** You do not register, and you never give us a name, an email address or a phone number.
- **Your diary lives on your device.** Meals, weight, water, workouts, progress photos, recipes, meal plans and your chats with the assistant are stored in the app on your iPhone or iPad. We do not hold a copy on our servers.
- **Some features have to send data out to work.** Photo recognition, voice logging, food search and the AI assistant send the relevant content to our server, which passes it to the service providers that carry out the processing. These requests are not stored after they are answered.
- **Apple Health data is never sent to our servers** and is never used for advertising.
- **We do not track you across apps or websites**, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
- **Deleting the app deletes your diary.** Because there is no account, there is nothing on our side to delete afterwards.

---

## 2. Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter into Bity is stored locally, in the app's own database on your device, and in the iOS backup of that device if you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup. Apple controls those backups, not us.

This includes:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Food diary | Meals and foods logged, portion sizes, calories, protein, carbs, fats, fiber, sugar, sodium, the meal they belong to, the date and time |
| Water and workouts | Amounts of water, workout type, duration, calories burned |
| Body data | Weight entries, height, date of birth or age, biological sex, activity level |
| Goals and preferences | Weight goal, calorie and macro targets, allergies, disliked foods, diet type, units, app appearance |
| Progress photos | Photos you take or pick to track your progress, stored as files inside the app |
| Recipes and plans | Saved recipes, created recipes, pantry items, meal plans |
| Assistant chats | Your conversation history with the in-app AI assistant |
| Progress and rewards | Streaks, experience points, unlocked badges |
| Widget and Live Activity data | A small daily summary (calories eaten and remaining, per-meal totals, streak) placed in a shared container on your device so the home screen widget and the Dynamic Island can display it |

We cannot read any of this. It is not transmitted to us, except where a specific feature you use sends part of it out, as described in section 4.

## 3. Apple Health

Health integration is **off by default** and only starts after you turn it on and grant permission in the iOS Health permission sheet. You can revoke it at any time in **Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices**, or turn it off inside Bity.

When enabled, Bity may **read**: body mass, height, date of birth, biological sex, step count, active energy burned, dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium and water.

When enabled, Bity may **write** back the food, water, weight and workouts you log in the app.

Health data is used only to keep your diary and your Health app in sync. In line with Apple's requirements:

- Health data is **never sent to our servers or to any third party**.
- Health data is **never used for advertising, marketing or similar services**.
- Health data is **never sold**, and is not disclosed to data brokers or information resellers.
- Health data is not used for the AI features described below unless the same values also exist in your Bity profile, which you entered yourself.

## 4. Information that leaves your device

Bity routes the following requests through our own server, which forwards them to the service providers that carry out the processing. They act only on our instructions, under written agreements. We route requests this way so that provider access keys are never shipped inside the app.

These requests carry **no account identifier, no device identifier, no name and no email**, because Bity has none.

**a) Meal photo recognition.** When you photograph a meal or pick a photo for analysis, the image is sent for recognition, together with the meal slot you chose and any note you typed. The image is processed to return an estimate and is not stored by us.

**b) Voice logging.** When you log food by voice, the audio recording is sent for transcription and the resulting text is analysed. The recording is not stored by us.

**c) Text analysis.** When you describe a food in writing, that text is sent for analysis.

**d) The AI assistant.** When you send a message to the assistant, we send your message together with the context the assistant needs to answer usefully. That context can include: your calorie, macro and water targets; today's totals and the meals you logged today; your profile values (biological sex, age, height, weight); your preferences (allergies, disliked foods, diet type, goal type); a recipe or meal plan you are looking at; and your device locale and time zone. Requests are stateless, so no conversation thread is kept on the provider's side. Your chat history is kept only on your device.

**e) Food and recipe search.** Search terms, barcodes you scan and recipe queries are sent to look up matching products and recipes.

**f) Analytics.** See section 5.

**g) Subscriptions.** See section 6.

## 5. Analytics

Bity collects anonymous usage statistics so we can understand how the app performs and keep improving it. These statistics are tied to a random identifier rather than to you, contain none of your diary entries, photos or Apple Health data, and are never used for advertising.

---

## 6. Subscriptions and payments

Bity offers auto-renewing subscriptions. Purchases are processed by **Apple**, and we manage entitlements through a third-party subscription management service.

From them we receive: whether you have an active subscription or trial, the product purchased, the expiry date and anonymous transaction identifiers.

**We never receive or store your card number, billing address or Apple ID password.**

---

## 7. Notifications

Bity's reminders (meals, water, weight, streaks) are **local notifications scheduled on your device**. There is no push server, and no notification content is sent over the network.

---

## 8. What we never collect

- Name, email address, phone number, postal address
- Contacts, calendar, precise or background location
- The advertising identifier (IDFA). Bity never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, so iOS withholds the identifier from the app and from every SDK inside it
- Any data used to build an advertising profile

---

## 9. Retention

- **On your device:** until you delete the entry or delete the app.
- **AI requests:** processed and discarded. Our server keeps no request log tied to you. Providers may retain requests briefly for abuse monitoring under their own policies, typically up to 30 days.
- **Subscription records:** kept as long as needed for the subscription and for tax and accounting obligations.

---

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent.

**Because Bity has no account, the fastest way to exercise most of these rights is directly in the app:**

- **Access and portability:** your data is on your device and visible in the app.
- **Correction:** edit or delete any entry.
- **Deletion:** delete the entry, or delete the app to remove everything.
- **Withdraw Health consent:** iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.
- **Object to analytics:** write to us at bity.calorie.tracker@gmail.com and we will exclude your device identifier.

For anything else, contact **bity.calorie.tracker@gmail.com**. We answer within 30 days.

---

## 11. Security

Network traffic uses TLS. Your diary is protected by your device's own encryption and passcode. Access keys for our providers are held on our server and never shipped inside the app.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Because your diary never reaches our servers, the most important protection is the passcode, Face ID or Touch ID on your device.

---

## 12. Bity is not a medical service

Bity estimates nutrition, including through AI, and those estimates can be wrong. Bity does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and is not a medical device. Talk to a qualified professional before making decisions about your health, especially if you are pregnant, have an eating disorder, diabetes or any other medical condition.
